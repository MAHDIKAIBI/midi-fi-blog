Its technical reputation rests on a pressure-gradient, double-membrane capsule design. Two thin gold-sputtered diaphragms sit mounted closely together inside a machined brass housing. In traditional designs, a wound-metal transformer introduces subtle saturation and dampens sharp transients.</p><p>Removing that physical transformer cuts manufacturing costs, but pure solid-state balancing passes high-frequency transients straight to the recorder without natural smoothing.
