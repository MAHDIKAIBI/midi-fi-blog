Long before corporate security escorted veteran assemblers into a North Carolina parking lot, <span data-semantic-id="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moog_Music">Moog Music</span> was quietly stuffing its premium instruments with pre-assembled imported circuit boards. In reality, that corporate structure was purely cosmetic. Executive leadership allocated forty nine percent of minority stock shares to the workforce while keeping a fifty one percent controlling stake. This engineered deadlock left workers holding stock that paid zero dividends without an outright sale of the company.</p><p>By twenty twenty two, the bench reality for technicians was brutal. Starting pay was capped at fourteen dollars and ten cents an hour, falling far short of the local living wage baseline of seventeen dollars and seventy cents. For that compensation, technicians had to tension keybed actions, hand test velocity responses, and fully calibrate eleven twenty two hundred dollar synthesis units every single shift.</p><p>In June of twenty twenty two, factory workers launched an International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers union drive. Aside from five thousand dollar Model D reissues and towering modular rigs, internal documentation revealed a clear secret. Suddenly, bedroom producers and keyboardists flooded retail channels with orders. Management mistook that artificial demand spike for permanent growth, walking straight into a logistics trap known as the bullwhip effect.</p><p>Executive leadership placed massive forward purchase orders for potentiometers, metal chassis components, and imported surface mount circuit boards. When live touring resumed and inflation surged, retail orders evaporated overnight. That sudden shift left millions of dollars in dead inventory stacked on the loading docks, freezing operational cash flow solid.</p><p>Facing a terminal financial chokehold by May twenty twenty three, executive leadership did the unthinkable. Executive strategy focused on stripping out domestic overhead, consolidating firmware onto shared platforms, and moving hardware manufacturing directly to high-efficiency overseas assembly lines.</p><p>The employee ownership structure vanished overnight. Stock plans were liquidated for lump sum cash payouts, stripping the factory floor of its remaining governance stake. Maintaining prime downtown real estate for foot traffic directly contradicted the lean logistics model enforced by the new parent company.</p><p>In March twenty twenty four, eviction became absolute as operations vacated Broadway Street entirely. It rolls off automated assembly lines across major Taiwanese manufacturing hubs.</p><p>Those offshore production lines deliver much tighter surface mount tolerances, strict component testing, unified USB MIDI, and stable digital to analog converter architecture. On your studio desk, modern units provide dependable keybed action, wide dynamic range, and rigid chassis construction. The famous ladder filter sound survives, even if the dedicated builders who spent decades hand assembling those instruments were permanently left behind.</p>
